PulseED · Quick Guide

ED Reassessment & Flow Station: keep one copy at every station

Live: pulseed.patientflowms.com
Training: pulseed.patientflowms.com/?training

1 · Sign in

  1. Open pulseed.patientflowms.com.
  2. Click Continue with Google and pick your own account. Never share a login: every action is recorded under your name.
  3. The station locks after 15 min idle. Sign in again; nothing is lost.

"Not on the staff list"? Ask the charge nurse to have you added.

Practise any time: the Training version link at the bottom of the left menu (or pulseed.patientflowms.com/?training) opens a safe copy with synthetic patients. No account needed.

2 · Admit a patient

  1. Click + New Patient Entry.
  2. Copy URN / MRN exactly from the EHR. Add name, complaint, CTAS and team.
  3. Tick one or more workflows: Vitals, Awaiting ED Bed (needs intended specialty), Awaiting Procedure.

A URN that is already active is blocked, so check All Patients first.

3 · Reassessments (Vitals)

Record each one with Record Reassessment. Colours: amber = due soon, red = overdue (chime repeats every 2 min). Overdue is logged automatically.

4 · Beds & procedures

  • Awaiting ED Bed: allocate a bed when one is assigned. A bed held by another patient is blocked unless you confirm an override.
  • Releasing a bed needs a reason.
  • Awaiting Procedure: update status: Awaiting → Ready → In Progress → Done (or Deferred / Cancelled), plus consent and arranged.
  • Waits turn amber at 60 min and red at 120 min.

5 · Exit (Global Exit Rule)

When the patient leaves the waiting area, click Record exit (or Place & Exit on the bed list) and choose how:

  • Placed on ED bed: enter the actual bed.
  • Sent directly to ward / unit · Discharged · LWBS.
  • Left against advice · Transferred · Deceased · Entered in error: reason required.

Exit closes every open workflow for that patient. Reopening someone else's exit (or after 10 min) needs a Charge Nurse.

6 · Charge nurses & leaders

  • Shift Handoff (SBAR): live summary for handover.
  • Admin & SPC Analytics: compliance (target 90%), waits, trends.
  • Event Audit Trail: who did what, when. Exports are logged too.
  • Shift report email with one PDF arrives at 06:00, 14:00 and 22:00 (doctors' shifts).
  • On the Main Dashboard, switch the shift view: Doctors · 8 h or Nursing · 12 h (07:00–19:00, 19:00–07:00).
  • Settings (Charge Nurse / ED Leader / Admin): intervals, beds, lists.
  • Analytics and the audit trail appear only for Charge Nurses, ED Leaders and Admins. Excel exports and the Google Sheet are for ED Leaders and Admins only.

Training: practise before your first live shift

Open pulseed.patientflowms.com/?training. It is full of synthetic patients and stays on that PC only; nothing reaches the live register. Use any name to sign in. Try each task once:

  1. Admit a CTAS 3 patient into Vitals + Awaiting ED Bed.
  2. Record a reassessment, then find an overdue patient and reassess them.
  3. Allocate a bed, then try to give the same bed to a second patient and read the warning.
  4. Move a procedure from Awaiting to Done.
  5. Exit a patient as "Placed on ED bed" and check all their workflows closed.
  6. Open Shift Handoff (SBAR) and the Event Audit Trail and find your own actions.

Never enter real patients in training. The orange TRAINING / DEMO MODE bar shows you are in training.
